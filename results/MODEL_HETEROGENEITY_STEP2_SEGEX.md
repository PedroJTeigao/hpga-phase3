# Model heterogeneity, step 2: is the modal crossover cut the prompt's number, or the model's?

*Branch `model-heterogeneity`. No GA, no fitness. The PHASE3_RESULTS.md §9.3 ablation, repeated on `llama3.2:3b`, `qwen2.5:7b` and `mistral:7b` (each reported on its own, nothing pooled). Driver `experiments/run_model_segex_ablation.py`; tables generated by `experiments/summarize_model_segex.py` from the raw files in `results/raw/` (`sequence_operator_compliance_<model>_len63_hetero_segex_<setting>.json`, `llm_operator_calls_hetero_segex_<setting>_<model>.jsonl`, console and driver logs). The tables below are the generated file `results/raw/model_segex_tables.md`, unedited.*

## Setup

Two settings per model, each a fresh run of the existing probe (`probe_sequence_operator_compliance.py`, unchanged, `--conditions crossover/segment --segment-example <setting>`): 100 calls, genome length 63, length bounds [30, 80], temperature 0.7, seed 0, `num_ctx` 4096, default retries, model loaded and warmed before its first setting.

- **a. `current`**: the prompt as shipped: worked example `0-40:1, 40-100:2`, prose sentence "a boundary at 40 falls 40% of the way along Parent 1 and 40% of the way along Parent 2".
- **b. `absent_prose25`**: worked example removed (prompt and retry hint) and the prose sentence rewritten with 25 in place of 40, so no 40 remains in the prompt template or retry hint.

Both settings restart the probe's RNG from seed 0, so where no call falls back they see the same parent pairs. The prompt check under each model's tables (from the logged prompts, every attempt) confirms the settings took effect: in `absent_prose25` no logged prompt contains the 0-40 example, the 40 prose or a "40" outside the Parent header lines; the only 40s left are parent lengths of 40 printed as data in a header (3, 5 and 8 attempts for llama3.2:3b, qwen2.5:7b and mistral:7b), which the template cannot avoid.

## What the tables say

**Does the modal cut move away from 40 when no 40 is in the prompt? Yes, in all three models, completely.** With the prompt as shipped every model's modal cut is 40, and 40 is in nearly every valid declaration (93 of 93 for llama3.2:3b, 100 of 100 for qwen2.5:7b, 95 of 96 for mistral:7b). With no 40 in the prompt, no cut equals 40 in any model (0 of 286, 0 of 112 and 0 of 159 cuts). No model keeps 40 without a 40 to copy. That includes the 3, 5 and 8 attempts in which a parent of length 40 is printed as data: none of them produced a 40 cut.

**Where the cut goes is the number left in the prompt, not the midpoint.** In `absent_prose25` the cut 25 appears in every valid declaration of every model (96 of 96, 100 of 100, 53 of 53), and it is the modal cut for `qwen2.5:7b` (89% of cuts) and, tied with 50, for `llama3.2:3b` (25, 50 and 75 in about a third of cuts each); for `mistral:7b` it is modal (33%) ahead of 50 and 75. `llama3.2:3b` almost always makes three cuts at 25, 50 and 75 (94 of 96 declarations have 4 segments); `mistral:7b` mostly makes 4 segments (51 of 53) with cuts at 25 (53 of 53 declarations), 50 (40 cuts) and 75 or 80 (28 and 22 cuts); `qwen2.5:7b` mostly makes one cut at 25 (89 of 100 declarations have two segments). This differs from `gemma4:12b`, which under the same setting chose 50 in 100 of 100 calls and never 25 (PHASE3_RESULTS.md §9.3). So the four models do not behave alike here: gemma4:12b ignores the illustrative number and falls back to the midpoint, the other three adopt it.

**Distinct cut values (of 99).** Setting a: 7, 1 and 11. Setting b: 3, 3 and 13. Removing 40 did not widen the range in llama3.2:3b or qwen2.5:7b. In mistral:7b the 13 values are 25, 50, 75 and 80 plus a scatter of small counts around 50 (45×4, 55×3, 49×2, one each of 48, 52, 53) and a few near 60 to 70, on only 53 valid declarations.

**Compliance is prompt-sensitive too, and hurt mistral:7b badly.** Fallback in setting a / b: llama3.2:3b 7/100 → 4/100, qwen2.5:7b 0/100 → 0/100, mistral:7b 4/100 → 47/100 (requests per call 1.18 → 2.24). The mistral:7b setting-b result therefore rests on 53 valid declarations, and 170 of its 171 rejected replies in setting b (20 of 22 in setting a) are segment lists that restart after reaching 100, with non-increasing start values (e.g. `0-25:1, 25-75:2, 75-100:1, 0-25:2, ...`), which the format forbids. Removing the worked example made this model much worse at the format.

## What this does and does not show

- The cut value follows the prompt. For all three models the modal cut is the prompt's illustrative number in both settings (40, then 25) and that number is in essentially every valid declaration. Combined with the step-1 result that every model's modal cut was 40, this supports reading the "40 collapse" as a property of the prompt, not of the models. The three models do not agree with gemma4:12b on what happens when the number is changed, which is a real between-model difference, but it is a difference in how closely they copy the prompt.
- **This design cannot separate the prose number from the worked example.** Setting b changes both at once (example removed, prose changed to 25). PHASE3_RESULTS.md §9.3 separated them for gemma4:12b (moving the example to 70 left the mode at 40; removing it left 40; only changing the prose number moved it). That was not done here, so for these three models we know that removing every 40 moves the cut to 25, not which of the two removed pieces was responsible, and we did not test any number other than 40 and 25.
- One run per setting, 100 calls, one seed, temperature 0.7. A rerun of the unchanged prompt (setting a) against step 1, which used the same prompt but a different RNG stream (mutate ran first), differed in the small numbers: `llama3.2:3b` fallback 2/100 vs 7/100, `mistral:7b` 13/100 vs 4/100 and 5 vs 11 distinct cuts, `qwen2.5:7b` identical. So differences of that size between runs are within sampling variation; the finding rests on the 40 to 25 shift and the 0 of 286/112/159 cuts at 40, which are far outside it.
